04 — Specification

Made to be read.

Case
41 mm, Grade 5 Titanium. Satin-brushed flanks, 45° hand-beveled mirror-polished anglage, ergonomic curved horn lugs, screw-down titanium crown with emerald cabochon.
Dial
Deep imperial midnight-blue sunburst finish. Applied faceted 18k champagne-gold indices with Super-LumiNova inlays, open-heart escapement exhibition aperture at 8 o'clock, astronomical moonphase at 6 o'clock, date complication at 3 o'clock.
Movement
Manufacture Calibre C-01. 28,800 vibrations per hour (4 Hz), 18k gold balance wheel with 16 regulating screws, blued balance spring, Côtes de Genève stripes, circular perlage, 22k gold winding rotor, 31 jewels.
Strap
Integrated articulated titanium multi-link bracelet with alternating longitudinal brushed H-links, mirror-polished champagne gold center facets, and dual-pusher safety deployant clasp.
